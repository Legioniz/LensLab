# LENS LAB — Photo Practice references

LENS LAB 0.5.1 includes 25 real-world reference photographs, grouped into five exercises that correspond to the simulator's existing scenes. The application loads the image renditions directly from Unsplash when the Photo Practice tab is used; image files are not redistributed inside this source archive.

## Categories

- Portraits — focus, depth of field, skin-tone exposure, and natural light.
- Landscapes — depth, horizon placement, highlight protection, and low ISO.
- Night scenes — metering, shutter speed, ISO, and bright-sign detail.
- Action & motion — freezing movement, tracking, and exposure trade-offs.
- Food & products — light direction, reflections, texture, and framing.

Each practice card links to its original Unsplash photo page and names its photographer. The displayed aperture, shutter, ISO, and focal-length values are suggested exercise starting points, not the actual settings or EXIF of the source photographs.

It does not permit selling unmodified images or compiling Unsplash images to recreate a competing stock-photo service. Review the current terms before redistributing or changing how the images are used:
